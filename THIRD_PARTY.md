## projectM presets

The files under `presets/` are modified MilkDrop-compatible presets originally distributed with the Arch Linux `projectm` package. Their filenames preserve the credited preset authors. Blackdrop adds small bass/mid/treble modulation equations and numeric ordering prefixes.

Historic MilkDrop presets were generally shared without explicit per-file licenses. The projectM preset project documents that individual authors may retain copyright and asks downstream users to remove a preset if an author objects. The repository MIT license applies to Blackdrop's original code and modifications, not to any third-party rights in these preset works.

Sources:

- https://github.com/projectM-visualizer/projectm
- https://github.com/projectM-visualizer/presets-cream-of-the-crop
